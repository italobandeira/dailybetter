Frequently asked
questions.

Didn't find your answer? Get in touch.

Is DailyBetter free?

Yes. The app is free, shows ads and has no subscription.

Do I have to log every day?

No. Log whenever it makes sense to you. More entries make patterns easier to see, but there is no daily goal and no penalty for skipping a day.

Can I log just my mood?

Yes. Picking a face is a complete entry. Notes, activities, sleep and energy are optional.

What if I switch phones?

Export a backup from the app and keep the file wherever you like, such as Google Drive. On your new phone, install DailyBetter and import that file. If Google backup is turned on in Android, the system may also restore the app automatically. See the step-by-step guide.

Does the app diagnose anything?

No. DailyBetter is a journal for personal reflection. Its charts only show what you logged and are not a substitute for professional assessment or advice.
