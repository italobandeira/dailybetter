Behind the app

DailyBetter grew from a simple idea: every day deserves room, the light ones, the hard ones and the ones in between. I want to build tools that help people listen to themselves a little more, one day at a time.

I read every message myself. Suggestions and stories about how you use the app help decide what to improve next. Replies are usually in Portuguese or English.

What would you like to talk about?

Pick a topic to open an email with the subject already filled in.

  • Questions about the appLogging, reminders, stats or themes.
  • Suggestions and feedbackFeature ideas or something that could be simpler.
  • Something didn't workErrors, backup or restore. Include your phone model and Android version.
  • Privacy and dataRequests about your personal data.

You don't need to send the contents of your journal. If a screenshot or a backup helps explain a problem, it will be used only to answer your message, as described in the Privacy Policy.
